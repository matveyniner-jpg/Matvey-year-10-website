It wasn't long after opening that John-Caleb Bradberton realized he wanted to share the joy of making Nuka-Cola with his audience. The local Bottling Plant was remodeled and updated to allow for a fun and educational experience that all ages can enjoy. Currently, the Bottling Plant is featuring Nuka-Cola's latest product, Nuka-Cola Quantum. The Bottling Plant is located northeast of Nuka-Town U.S.A and north of Kiddie Kingdom.
