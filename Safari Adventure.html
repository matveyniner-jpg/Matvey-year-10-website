Safari Adventure was the largest undertaking by John-Caleb Bradberton. It took a grand total of 7 years to build thanks to the valuable input from a variety of zoological and environmental scientists. It finally opened its gates during the Labor Day weekend of 2067, 9 years after Dry Rock Gulch. Popular attractions at Safari Adventure include Cappy's Treehouse, the Primate House, and the Reptile House. And please mind our dust as we work hard to bring you future entertainment with the Angry Anaconda Rollercoaster. Coming soon in 2078! Safari Adventure is located north of Nuka-Town U.S.A.
